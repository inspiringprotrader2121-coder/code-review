Candidate findings are challenged

The verifier checks premises against source context and removes findings it can factually refute.

02 · COVERAGE

Fingerprints and source anchors connect re-reviews, suppress duplicate posts, and identify findings fixed by later commits.

// FAQ

Plans, limits, and billing.

The practical details: what consumes usage, monthly totals, and hourly caps.

How does an Orvex review work?

When a pull request opens or receives a new push, deterministic checks run first. Two or four focused AI passes (by review track) then inspect correctness, security, reliability, performance, and contract edges. Orvex merges duplicate findings and strictly verifies the evidence before posting.

What commands can I run on a pull request?

Comment @orvex help for the full list, or see Commands. Common ones: @orvex review, @orvex deep, @orvex fix, @orvex fix all, @orvex rate limit, and thread replies like @orvex explain / @orvex ignore. After install, the dashboard Settings page lists every command for your workspace.

What counts as one review?

A completed standard pull-request review uses one review unit. An @orvex deep review uses two units because it performs additional analysis. Skipped reviews, fix commands, and explanation commands do not consume review units; failed reviews still count toward free-trial, hourly, and monthly caps. A fresh review after a new push counts as a new review.

What happens when I use my monthly reviews?

On Starter, Verify Lite, and Verify, buy prepaid credits in the Orvex dashboard after the included allotment. Reviews draw from that wallet until it is empty or the hard monthly ceiling is reached. Free stops after 10 lifetime reviews; Pro stops at its hard monthly total. Comment @orvex rate limit anytime to see remaining capacity.

Why does every plan have an hourly limit?

Monthly totals cap overall spend. Hourly and concurrent caps prevent accidental review loops and keep processing reliable across every workspace.

Does the Free allowance reset?

No. Free includes 10 lifetime reviews per GitHub account at up to 2 per hour, with no card required. Creating another workspace or reinstalling the GitHub App does not reset the allowance.

Do lower-priced plans get a weaker pipeline?

No. Every plan gets the full pipeline for its review track: deterministic checks, two or four focused review passes, strict finding verification, finding memory, and autofix. Verify-track plans also include a sandboxed investigate pass; paid plans include on-demand deep review. Plans otherwise differ by review track, monthly allowance, hourly rate limit, queue priority, and support.

Is pricing per developer seat?

No. Billing is per workspace, not per developer. Only a workspace owner can start or change billing. Workspace members can use the review and dashboard features without purchasing another seat.

When does the monthly allowance reset?

Included review units use a rolling 30-day window (not calendar billing-period reset). Hourly limits are separate burst controls and continue to apply. Prepaid wallet credits do not expire with the rolling window.

How do I cancel or get billing help?

Email support@useorvex.com from the address on your Orvex account and include the workspace name. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period.

What languages and repositories can Orvex review?

Orvex can review text-based GitHub pull-request diffs across common programming languages. Language-specific deterministic checks run where applicable, and supported text diffs receive the focused AI review pipeline.
