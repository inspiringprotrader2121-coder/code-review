Refund Policy

Last updated: 19 July 2026

We want trying Orvex to be risk-free — that's why every account starts with free reviews before any payment is taken. This policy explains how refunds work once you subscribe.

1. 14-day guarantee on your first subscription

If you are not satisfied with your first subscription purchase of any paid plan, email support@useorvex.com within 14 days of the first charge and we will refund it in full. This applies once per workspace, to the first billing period only.

2. Renewals and cancellation

  • Subscriptions renew monthly. To cancel, email support@useorvex.com from your account address and include the workspace name. Your plan stays active until the end of the paid period and is not charged again.
  • Renewal charges are generally non-refundable, but if you cancelled before a renewal and were still charged, or you contact us within 72 hours of an unwanted renewal with no reviews used in the new period, we will refund it.
  • We do not give partial or pro-rata refunds for unused time or unused included reviews.

3. Prepaid overage credits

Prepaid wallet top-ups pay for reviews past your included monthly allotment. Unused credit balance remaining after a Stripe refund of the original top-up payment may be clawed back. Credits already spent on delivered reviews are non-refundable — except where the reviews were the result of a malfunction on our side (see below).

4. Billing errors and service failures

  • Duplicate charges, charges after cancellation, and other billing errors are always refunded in full.
  • If a service outage or defect on our side consumed your included reviews or generated overage (for example, a runaway re-review loop that was our fault), we will credit or refund the affected amount.

5. EU / UK consumers

By starting a subscription you request immediate access to a digital service. Depending on where you live and whether service has begun, that request may affect a statutory cancellation or withdrawal right. Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under applicable consumer law, and the guarantee in section 1 applies in addition to those rights.

6. How to request a refund

Email support@useorvex.com from the address on your account with the workspace name. Refunds are processed to the original payment method via Stripe, normally within 5–10 business days.
