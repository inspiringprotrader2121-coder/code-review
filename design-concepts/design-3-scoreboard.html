The receipts

What “catch rate” actually means.

We ran Orvex head-to-head against other review bots on 80 real pull requests. The story is coverage: more models, more passes, more real bugs surfaced.

01 Defect-cluster catch rate

Every PR has a known set of real defects. Catch rate is the share of those defect clusters a reviewer flagged. Orvex hit 43% — ahead of Codex (29%), CodeRabbit (28%) and Qodo (22%).

02 Head-to-head, same PRs

Same 80 pull requests, same defects, every bot judged against the same ground truth. No cherry-picking — a straight, comparable run.

03 Coverage, honestly framed

This is a coverage win, not a precision claim. More models and more passes surface more real bugs — including ones the others missed entirely.
